# Product

## Who it is for
Robotics professors and lab admissions staff in Japan, and anyone hiring for robotics research or internships.

## The one goal
In under a minute, a visitor knows who Idris Sadiq is, sees one thing he built that impresses them, and knows how to contact him.

## Two modes
- **Calm** is the portfolio: six sections (hero, About, Work, Path, Beyond the lab, Contact). Each project has its own page at `#work/<slug>`. Experience mode: the work and the person lead; the interface recedes.
- **Chaos** is a second version of the same portfolio, reached only through the "One more thing" section. Loud, cartoon, full of secrets. It must still show every project, the About text, the CV and the contact details, and its secrets lead people into the projects.

## Voice
Idris's own. See `docs/VOICE.md`. About keeps his wording and contractions. Everything else: plain, first person, accurate, no hype.

## Rules that do not bend
- Never invent facts. Unknown things are `TODO(idris)`, hidden on the live site, and listed in `docs/ANSWERS-NEEDED.md`.
- Calm stays professional: no popups, no sound, no comments because of Chaos.
- Accessibility: reduced motion honoured everywhere, tap targets at least 44 px, no horizontal scroll from 360 px wide.
